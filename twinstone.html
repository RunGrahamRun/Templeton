Twinstone ranks corroboration evidence, not operational targets. A high score means multiple recent evidence classes are spatially/temporally coherent and of usable source quality. It does not establish cause, intent, attribution, military significance or future activity.
Investigation notebook
No evidence pinned yet. Use “Pin to notebook” from a map popup.
Notebook notes and assessments are analyst-authored material. They are not source observations and are never promoted to corroborating evidence merely because they are stored beside evidence references.
Investigation context
Context is kept epistemically separate from map evidence. IODA describes connectivity disruption; NOAA SWPC is global space-environment context; ReliefWeb and WHO are reported-information sources. Context does not automatically increase Twinstone corroboration scores or establish cause.
